FAQ

1. How is Linky different from LinkedIn Premium AI or GPT?

LinkedIn's AI and standard GPT-based tools rely on static profile data, leading to generic "I see you're a founder" messages. Linky uses Claude Sonnet to analyze the specific context of a comment. It understands the nuances of a conversation, making your outreach feel like a real human-to-human follow-up, not a template.

2. Is there a risk of my LinkedIn account being banned?

No. Linky is a "Human-in-the-loop" tool. It doesn't automate the sending process (which is what LinkedIn's bots detect). It simply assists you in writing the draft. You are always the one clicking "Send," keeping your account 100% compliant with LinkedIn's Terms of Service.

3. Can I customize how the AI writes?

Absolutely. Unlike other "black-box" AI tools, Linky gives you a Custom Rules box. You can tell it to "keep it under 3 lines," "avoid corporate jargon," or "always end with a specific question." Claude reads your rules before generating every single word.

4. Does it work with Sales Navigator?

Yes. Linky is designed to work where you work. Whether you are browsing your standard feed or doing deep prospecting on Sales Navigator, the "Save to Library" and "Generate" features will be right there with you.
